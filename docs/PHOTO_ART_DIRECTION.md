## 1. Photographic Manifesto

House of Otaru garments are defined by **weight, fiber integrity, natural dye depth, and human construction**. 

When a luxury customer views an online garment, they evaluate the authenticity of the house through the camera lens. Synthetic AI imagery, plastic skin tones, distorted garment seams, and unrelated stock photos destroy collector trust instantly.

### Core Visual Directives:
1. **Natural, Unhurried Northern Light**: All studio and campaign imagery is captured using diffuse natural daylight (diffuse overcast daylight or soft window sidelight). Avoid artificial strobe flashes, colored gels, or glossy studio reflections.
2. **Tactile Texture Priority**: The weave must be tactilely legible. The camera must capture the physical slub of 14.5oz raw shuttle-loom canvas, the subtle marbling of paraffin wax on cotton, and the deep botanical saturation of sukumo indigo.
3. **Restrained Human Presence**: Models are captured in natural, contemplative postures. Avoid dramatic commercial poses, forced smiles, or theatrical streetwear stunts. The human form is the scaffold for the garment, not the distraction.
4. **Authentic Color Fidelity**: Colors are graded to match physical fabric samples under 5000K neutral light. No hyper-saturated cyan indigo or artificial orange flares.

## 3. Detailed Shot Specifications

### Slot 01: The Signature Campaign Hero (`hero-campaign.jpg`)
- **Subject**: Model wearing the **Object 041 Yama Field Jacket** over a natural unbleached raw cotton tunic.
- **Environment**: Quiet architectural interior overlooking a cold harbour horizon or textured concrete wall with natural plaster patina.
- **Lighting**: Diffuse morning side-light coming from camera left. Soft natural drop shadows; warm highlight skimming across the shoulder seam.
- **Composition**: Rule of thirds; model positioned slightly right of center, looking into the negative space. Left 40% of the composition is calm, low-contrast negative space reserved for the campaign typography.
- **Camera Perspective**: Eye-level, 50mm or 85mm prime lens, f/4.0 to maintain crisp focal clarity from lapel to wrist cuff.
- **Color Grading**: Deep botanical indigo blues (`#1F2A44`), warm cream undertones (`#F5F2EC`), matte black shadows (`#111110`).
- **Responsive Framing**:
  - **Desktop (16:9)**: Horizontal frame; model on right, typography clears cleanly on left.
  - **Mobile (9:16)**: Vertical vertical crop focused from collarbone to mid-thigh, keeping the garment silhouette dominant and headline positioned at top.

### Slot 02: Object 041 — Yama Field Jacket Merchandising Suite
1. **Primary Product Shot (`041-primary.jpg`)**:
   - **Ratio**: 4:5 vertical (`1200 × 1500px`).
   - **Styling**: Garment buttoned to the second horn button, laid flat on an aged limestone slab or draped on a minimalist blackened-steel tailor’s hanger. Natural relaxed drape through the sleeves.
   - **Focal Point**: Collar construction, blackened brass hardware, and heavy selvedge placket.
2. **Secondary On-Body Study (`041-secondary.jpg`)**:
   - **Ratio**: 4:5 vertical.
   - **Styling**: Model standing 3/4 turn in natural shade, hands relaxed in dual-entry bellows pockets. Shows true length (hits mid-hip) and shoulder drop.
3. **Tactile Hardware Detail (`041-detail.jpg`)**:
   - **Ratio**: 4:5 vertical.
   - **Framing**: Macro crop of the collar stand showing triple-needle chainstitch in unbleached ecru thread against deep indigo twill.
4. **Macro Weave Scan (`041-macro.jpg`)**:
   - **Ratio**: 1:1 square (`1000 × 1000px`).
   - **Framing**: Extreme close-up (100mm macro) showing the irregular yarn slubs and botanical sukumo pigment clinging to the warp threads.

### Slot 03: Object 042 — Kiryū Wrap Trouser Merchandising Suite
- **Primary Shot (`042-primary.jpg`)**: 4:5 vertical layout showing the asymmetric wrap-front closure, natural herringbone interior ties, and wide architectural leg taper.
- **On-Body Motion Shot (`042-secondary.jpg`)**: Model walking slowly to capture the fluid drape and matte crepe texture of the sandwash silk-hemp blend.

### Slot 04: Studio & Provenance Photography (`studio-hands.jpg`)
- **Subject**: Close-up of artisan hands at an oak cutting table using traditional brass shears and chalk, or binding a boro indigo patch with white sashiko thread.
- **Environment**: Real atelier workspace. Wooden tables, spools of linen thread, bone folders.
- **Lighting**: Warm 45-degree desk lamp or window daylight illuminating the craft tools.
- **Integrity Invariant**: Strictly depict genuine garment construction and textile preparation. Zero staged props, fake ancient scrolls, or fabricated vintage equipment.
